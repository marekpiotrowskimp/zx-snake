zxSnake - Classic Snake Game for ZX Spectrum 48K
=================================================

zxSnake is a faithful reimagining of the classic snake game, crafted specifically 
for the ZX Spectrum 48K. Featuring colorful 16x16 pixel graphics, animated fruits, 
and 8 unique challenging levels, it brings the timeless gameplay to Sir Clive's 
beloved machine.

Guide your snake through increasingly difficult mazes, collecting fruits to grow 
longer and rack up points. Each fruit type offers different rewards - from the 
common apple worth 10 points per level, to the elusive cherry that grants a 
whopping 25 points multiplied by the current level number.

FEATURES:

* 8 Hand-Crafted Levels - From open fields to complex mazes, each level presents 
  unique challenges with different wall layouts.

* 4 Animated Fruit Types - Apples, bananas, grapes, and cherries, each with their 
  own point values and smooth 2-frame animations.

* New Game+ Mode - Think you've mastered all 8 levels? Complete them and start 
  again with increased speed and more fruits required. The difficulty keeps 
  scaling infinitely!

* 3 Lives System - Make mistakes without instant game over. Each death returns 
  you to the start of the current level with your score preserved from when you 
  entered it.

* Multiple Control Options - Choose between QAOP, WASD keyboard layouts, or 
  Kempston joystick - whatever feels most comfortable.

* Authentic Presentation - Custom loading screen, catchy menu music, and 
  satisfying sound effects for eating, dying, and leveling up.

* Polished Snake Graphics - Proper directional head sprites, corner pieces for 
  smooth turns, and animated tail - not just simple blocks!

The game runs on any ZX Spectrum 48K or compatible machine, including 128K models 
and modern emulators. Written entirely in Z80 assembly language for optimal 
performance.

Challenge yourself to beat all 8 levels and see how many New Game+ loops you can 
survive as the snake gets faster with each completion. Can you reach the top of 
the high score table?

Perfect for quick gaming sessions or extended play - zxSnake delivers that 
"just one more try" feeling that made classic arcade games so addictive.
