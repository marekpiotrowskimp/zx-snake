CONTROLS
--------
Select your preferred control scheme in the main menu:

  Option 1 (QAOP):    Q = Up, A = Down, O = Left, P = Right
  Option 2 (WASD):    W = Up, S = Down, A = Left, D = Right
  Option 3 (Kempston): Joystick directions

Press 1, 2, or 3 in the menu to start the game with your chosen controls.


HOW TO PLAY
-----------
Guide your snake around the board to eat fruits. Each fruit collected makes
your snake grow longer and adds points to your score.

- Collect the required number of fruits to advance to the next level
- Avoid hitting walls and your own tail
- You start with 3 lives
- Losing a life resets your score to what it was at the start of the level


FRUITS & SCORING
----------------
Different fruits have different point values, multiplied by the current level:

  Apple   (Red)      10 x level points
  Banana  (Yellow)   15 x level points
  Grapes  (Magenta)  20 x level points
  Cherry  (Red)      25 x level points

You also earn 1 point for each move you survive.


LEVELS
------
The game features 8 unique levels with different wall layouts:

  Level 1: Empty       - Open field, no obstacles
  Level 2: Border      - Walls around the edges
  Level 3: Center      - Block in the middle
  Level 4: Corners     - Blocks in each corner
  Level 5: Cross       - Cross-shaped walls
  Level 6: Diamonds    - Diamond pattern obstacles
  Level 7: Pillars     - Vertical pillars
  Level 8: Maze        - Complex maze layout

After completing all 8 levels, the game enters "New Game+" mode - you return
to level 1 but with increased speed and more fruits required per level.
The difficulty keeps increasing with each loop!

REQUIREMENTS
------------
- ZX Spectrum 48K or compatible (128K, +2, +3, clones)
- Works with any ZX Spectrum emulator (FUSE, Spectaculator, ZX Spin, etc.)

TIPS
----
- The snake speeds up as you progress through levels and collect fruits
- Plan your route ahead - longer snakes are harder to maneuver
- Cherry gives the most points but appears less frequently
- In New Game+ mode, the snake starts faster - good reflexes required!
